Stop making the rounds of journal dashboards.

Most submission systems log you out the moment you blink. Paper Trail does the rounds for you — one click signs in to every journal and reads your statuses straight off the page.

1

Track a paper

Give it the manuscript's title and pick the journal. Covers, IDs, and submission dates fill themselves in.

2

Add your journal login — once

Entered on the paper's own card, encrypted into your device's secure storage. It never leaves your machine.

3

Click “Refresh all”

Paper Trail signs in to each journal, reads every manuscript's live status, and updates the trail — with a notification when anything changes.

Built around the way review actually feels.

Long silences, opaque statuses, and the eternal question of where to send it next. Each card answers the questions you actually have.

The trail

A living timeline

Submission → review → decision as one line, with a gently pulsing marker while reviewers are working. Holding states stay still; decisions turn the final node green, amber, or red.

Expected decisions

An honest countdown

Your submission date plus the journal's real average review time gives an expected decision date — counting down day by day, and counting up, in red, once it runs past the average.

Journal facts

Sourced, not folklore

Acceptance rates and wait times from publisher metrics and community surveys — shown side by side when they disagree, each linked to its source.

Where next?

Plan the resubmission

Rank candidate journals by decision speed or selectivity for your paper's word count — with its current home and past rejecters greyed out automatically.

The nudge

A polite status inquiry

Once a paper is well past the average, one click drafts the courteous chase-up email — manuscript ID, dates, and all. Copy it, or open it straight in your mail app.

The journey

Every paper's biography

Rejections carry forward when you resubmit, so each card can tell the whole story: where a paper has been, and how long each stop took.

Also in the box: co-author bylines · word-limit checker for 25+ philosophy journals · CSV export of your submission history · expected-decision dates as a calendar feed · automatic updates.

Private by architecture, not by promise.

Paper Trail is a desktop app in the old-fashioned sense: your data lives on your machine.

  • Your logins never leave your device. Journal passwords are encrypted into the operating system's secure storage — macOS Keychain, Windows DPAPI — and used only to sign in from your computer, the way you would.
  • No account. No cloud copy of your manuscripts. Statuses are read from your journals directly to your screen; nothing is stored anywhere else.
  • Community data is opt-in and inspectable. If — and only if — you switch it on, a concluded submission contributes one anonymous record to shared journal statistics. The app shows you the exact record before you ever opt in.
